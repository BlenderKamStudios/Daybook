Weekly free time

Thursday, Friday and Saturday are the big days. Sunday is blocked. Tasks are stacked from your start time, and a task that runs past your free hours gets a red dashed outline on the calendar.

  • Free time per week is 24 h: Mon and Wed 3 h, Tue 2 h, then Thu 5 h, Fri 6 h and Sat 5 h. Sunday is off, with nothing scheduled.
  • On 2–3 h days, do the theory block first. Shooting comes after.
  • Enter the contest on Sat 31 Oct, but only if assessments #2 and #3 are both 85% or higher. Otherwise push it a week and repeat Week 3.
  • Thursday to Saturday carry the heavy lifting: assessments, long shoots and write-ups land there.
  • Charge batteries and clear memory cards the night before every shoot.
  • Missed a day? Don't skip it. Shrink it, and log what you did.
